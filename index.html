Our Mission

Our Mission

In the city of Philadelphia, safe and supportive postoperative transitional housing options for Trans-Male and Trans-Female individuals remain extremely limited, and in many cases, unavailable.

MIIKKO.INC was founded from a deeply personal experience and a commitment to creating change where support systems have fallen short. After witnessing firsthand the challenges faced by a loved one recovering from postoperative surgery - including harassment, bullying, threats, theft, and unsafe shelter conditions - it became clear that there was a critical need for a clean, secure, and affirming space dedicated to recovery and stability.

Our organization exists to provide temporary transitional residency and supportive resources for Trans-Male and Trans-Female individuals recovering from surgery and navigating vulnerable stages of transition. We are committed to creating an environment built on dignity, safety, compassion, and respect.

More About Us

More About Us

In addition to housing support, MIIKKO.INC connects participants with valuable community resources, including financial assistance programs, permanent housing opportunities, educational support, and career development services.

Through advocacy, care, and community partnership, our mission is to help individuals heal, rebuild, and move forward with confidence and stability.
